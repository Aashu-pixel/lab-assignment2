SOME BIG LIFE AVICES FROM VARIOUS SUBJECTS

>
  • Physics says everything is relative, Buddhism says suffering is relative too, so stop treating every small problem like it has infinite magnitude.
  • Chemistry teaches that not every reaction is reversible, so in life learn to let go of people and situations that are not meant to return.
  • Math teaches us not to overanalyze every variable, so stop analyzing every text, look, and “k” someone sends you.
  • Computer Science teaches garbage in, garbage out, so if you constantly feed your mind negative thoughts, don't expect enlightenment.exe to run properly.
  • Physics teaches that an object stays at rest until an external force acts on it, so sometimes you have to become your own external force and get yourself moving.
  • Chemistry teaches equilibrium, and life also needs balance between studying, sleeping, relaxing, and occasionally touching grass.
  • Math teaches that every function has a domain, so remember that you also need boundaries and not everyone deserves access to your time and energy.
  • Computer Science teaches us not to run unnecessary processes, so stop running 47 imaginary conversations in your head at 2 AM.
  • Physics teaches that friction opposes motion, and in life fear and overthinking can be the biggest friction stopping you from moving forward.
  • Chemistry teaches that bonds can break, and Buddhism teaches us to appreciate relationships without assuming that they must last forever.
  • Math teaches limits, and life teaches that you can get close to something without necessarily making it yours, because sometimes the journey itself is the lesson.
  • Computer Science teaches us to save our work regularly, and life teaches us to save good memories without constantly reopening old files that crash our emotional operating system.
